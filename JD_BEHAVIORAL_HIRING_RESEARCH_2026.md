5. **Bias for Action** — Speed matters; calculated risk-taking
6. **Dive Deep** — Stay connected to details, audit frequently
7. **Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit** — Challenge respectfully, then commit
8. **Deliver Results** — Focus on key inputs, deliver with quality

## 7. TOP 5 PREPARATION STRATEGIES FROM SUCCESSFUL CANDIDATES

### Strategy 1: "The Consistent Grinder" (Most Recommended)
**Source:** Multiple successful Google/Amazon candidates on LeetCode, Medium, Reddit

**Core Principle:** Consistency beats intensity. 1 problem/day > 10 problems on Sunday.

**Execution:**
- Solve 1 LeetCode problem daily (no zero days)
- Use TakeUForward SDE Sheet or NeetCode 150 as the base
- Focus on Medium difficulty (70% medium, 20% hard, 10% easy)
- After solving, watch/read the optimal solution even if you got it right
- Track patterns, not individual problems
- **Target: 200-300 problems over 3 months** focusing on: Arrays, Strings, Trees, Graphs (DFS/BFS/Topo Sort/Dijkstra), DP (1D/2D/3D), Sliding Window, Two Pointers, Binary Search, Heaps

### Strategy 2: "The System Design First-Principles Approach"
**Source:** Successful SDE-2 candidates at Amazon, Flipkart, Razorpay

**Core Principle:** Design first by yourself before watching/reading solutions.

**Execution:**
- For every design problem, write your own FR/NFR first
- Draw your own architecture diagram BEFORE looking at solutions
- Then compare with standard approaches (Alex Xu Book 1 & 2)
- Practice out loud — talk through decisions as if explaining to an interviewer
- Cover 15-20 designs across: URL shortener, chat system, notification system, payment system, rate limiter, cache system, search engine, newsfeed, file storage, video streaming
- **Time allocation: 10-15 hours/week** split across HLD, LLD, and DSA

### Strategy 3: "The PR & Documentation Review Strategy"
**Source:** SDE-2 interview prep guides (workat.tech, preplaced.in)

**Core Principle:** Your work experience is your biggest weapon — mine it systematically.

**Execution:**
- Review ALL your PRs and documentation from last 6 months at GSTN
- For each significant PR, note: problem solved, technical approach, alternatives considered, outcome, metrics
- Map each experience to behavioral frameworks (Amazon LPs, Google Googleyness, etc.)
- Identify 12-15 strong stories that each map to 3-4 different behavioral questions
- Practice telling each story in under 3 minutes using STAR+L (add Learning at the end)

**Jayanti-Specific:** You already have 22 STAR stories in Amazon_LP_STAR_Bank.md. Cross-reference them against the 30 behavioral questions in Section 3 above. Fill gaps.

### Strategy 4: "The Mock Interview Circuit"
**Source:** Successful Google, Amazon, DoorDash candidates

**Core Principle:** Solving problems alone is different from solving them under interview pressure.

**Execution:**
- Start mock interviews by Week 3-4 of prep (don't wait until you feel "ready")
- Do 2-3 mock interviews per week minimum
- Use: Pramp (free), interviewing.io, Exponent, or find study partners on LeetCode Discord
- Record yourself if possible — review for "umm", long silences, jumping to code too fast
- Alternate between: coding mocks, system design mocks, behavioral mocks
- After each mock, write down: what went wrong, what went right, what to improve

### Strategy 5: "The Service-to-Product Translation Framework"
**Source:** Multiple Medium posts from service company engineers who cracked FAANG

**Core Principle:** Reframe service company experience using product company vocabulary.

**Execution:**
- NEVER say: "I worked on maintenance/support" → SAY: "I owned the reliability of a system serving 14M users"
- NEVER say: "My manager told me to" → SAY: "I identified the problem, proposed a solution, and drove implementation"
- Translate your work into IMPACT: latency numbers, throughput, users affected, cost savings
- Learn to speak in terms of: "tradeoffs", "scalability", "system boundaries", "failure modes"
- For Jayanti specifically:
  - "Maintained cache" -> "Redesigned DistCacheUtil for 14M taxpayers, achieving 85%+ hit ratio"
  - "Worked on Kafka" -> "Built org-wide Kafka consumer framework with DLQ, adopted by 6+ teams"
  - "Database work" -> "Implemented CQRS pattern reducing query latency from 8-12s to under 1.5s for 3B invoices/year"

| Ambiguous situation | Check Invent & Simplify | Google Googleyness framing needed |
| Customer pain point | Story A & B in Customer Obsession | Well covered |
| Mentoring someone | Check Hire & Develop | Need specific mentoring outcome story |
| Pushed back on stakeholder | Check Have Backbone | Well covered |
| Why this company? | N/A | Research each target company mission/product before interview |
